Here's what you need to know before then.

## Why

There are other companies and projects called Superset, and sharing a name has caused confusion for a while. We want a name that's fully ours, so we're changing it now, while we're still early.

## What changes

- Our name and logo.
- Our website and email move to a new domain. superset.sh keeps working and points you to the new one.

## What doesn't change

- The product. Same app, same features, same team.
- Your account, workspaces, settings, agents and billing.
- Your links. Old superset.sh links, docs links and integrations keep working.

## Do I need to do anything?

No. The desktop app picks up the new name on its own, and the `superset` command in your terminal keeps working.

## When

We'll reveal the new name later this month, together with some other news. Follow along on [X](https://x.com/superset_sh), [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/superset-sh) or [Discord](https://discord.gg/cZeD9WYcV7) to hear it first.
